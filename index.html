Late Night Bumper Playlist Generator

A minimalist, browser-local bumper sequencer. The canvas is the source of truth for preview, playback, and WebM export.

How It Works

Playback renders each bumper frame-by-frame onto the canvas, using the playlist order, durations, text layout, and fade transitions.

Export records the same canvas with captureStream() and MediaRecorder. When supported, enabled audio is mixed into the export stream and trimmed at video end.

Target browser: modern Chromium. WebM export is the practical browser-native format here; MP4 is not attempted client-side.

Project data restores from localStorage on refresh. Uploaded audio files do not persist across reloads, so reattach them after reopening.
